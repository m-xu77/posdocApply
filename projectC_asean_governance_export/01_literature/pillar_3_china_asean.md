# Pillar 3 — China–ASEAN Relations, with CLMV Emphasis

**Role in project**: regional grounding. Lets us interpret country-variation findings without claiming area-studies expertise we do not have.

---

## Core references

### Sutter, R.G. (2024 or latest). *China's Rise and Foreign Policy of East and Southeast Asia* (current edition).

- **Use**: textbook background; check ASEAN sections.

### Goh, E. (2019). "Contesting hegemonic order: China in East Asia." *Security Studies* 28 (3): 614–644.

- **Argument**: ASEAN states' hedging strategies vis-à-vis China.
- **Use**: motivates the regime-alignment vs. dependency contrast in H5.

### Ciorciari, J.D. & Haacke, J. (2019). "Hedging in international relations: an introduction." *International Relations of the Asia-Pacific* 19 (3): 367–374.

- **Argument**: framework for understanding CLMV positioning.
- **Use**: country-level interpretation of H4 results.

### Strangio, S. (2020). *In the Dragon's Shadow: Southeast Asia in the Chinese Century*. Yale UP.

- **Use**: country-chapter background for each of CLMV; useful for interpretation.

### Heng, P.B. (2012). "Cambodia–China relations: a positive-sum game?" *Journal of Current Southeast Asian Affairs* 31 (2): 57–85.

- **Use**: Cambodia-specific background; reception of Chinese development engagement.

### Hai Hong Nguyen (2024 or recent). "Vietnam's response to BRI: cautious engagement." (verify cite)

- **Use**: Vietnam-specific background; one of the two "hedging" cases in our CLMV set.

### Steinberg, D.I. & Fan, H. (2012). *Modern China–Myanmar Relations*. NIAS Press.

- **Use**: Myanmar background; useful pre-2021 baseline.

### Stuart-Fox, M. (2009). "Laos: the Chinese connection." *Southeast Asian Affairs* 2009: 141–169.

- **Use**: Laos background.
